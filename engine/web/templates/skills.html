VersionTaxonomy version and retrieval date stamped on every skill

Priority score

40 percent demand (share of postings, normalized 0 to 5) + 30 percent SME criticality + 20 percent credential weight (mentions in the exam objectives) + 10 percent entry-level fit. Core 3.5 and up, Supporting 2.5 to 3.4, Awareness below 2.5. Without licensed demand the other three are reweighted.
